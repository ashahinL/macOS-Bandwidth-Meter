Bandwidth Meter @VERSION@

Weekly and monthly bandwidth totals from the daily history Bandwidth+ already keeps.

This installs:

  • Bandwidth Meter.app in /Applications, the menu bar dashboard
  • the bandwidth-meter command in /usr/local/bin, for Terminal and scripts

Keep Bandwidth+ from the Mac App Store running so each day gets recorded. Bandwidth Meter only reads its files.

Updating? A running copy is quit and replaced. Your saved totals and settings stay where they are.
