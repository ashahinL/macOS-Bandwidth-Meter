Bandwidth Meter is installed

Look for this month's total, such as 67.5G, in the menu bar. Click it to open the panel. Settings are behind the gear.

If it isn't there, open Bandwidth Meter from Applications.

In Terminal, bandwidth-meter prints the same totals. bandwidth-meter --help lists the options.

To remove it later, open the disk image again and double-click Uninstall Bandwidth Meter.
