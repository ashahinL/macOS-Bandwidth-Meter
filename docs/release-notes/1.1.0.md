Bandwidth Meter 1.1.0 installs from one disk image, with no Terminal. Updating
from 1.0.x keeps everything you have recorded.

**Install**
- Open `Bandwidth-Meter-1.1.0.dmg` and double-click **Install.pkg**. It puts
  the menu bar app in `/Applications` and the `bandwidth-meter` command in
  `/usr/local/bin`, then opens the app. When you close the installer window,
  the disk image is ejected.
- If Bandwidth+ isn't installed, the installer says so and links to it on the
  Mac App Store. You can continue: **Import CSV** works without it.
- **Uninstall.app** on the same disk image removes the app, the command, and
  the installer receipt. It asks before it deletes your saved totals, and
  keeps them unless you say otherwise.

**First launch**
- The installer isn't notarized yet, so macOS stops it the first time. On
  macOS 15 and later, click **Open Anyway** in **System Settings › Privacy &
  Security**. The steps are under Download below.

**Also**
- The installer quits a running copy before it updates, and keeps your history
  and settings.
- The three zips and `install.sh` from 1.0.1 are gone. If you used the CLI zip,
  the old command in `~/.local/bin` may come first on your `PATH`. Delete it,
  or run Uninstall.app, which removes it too.
