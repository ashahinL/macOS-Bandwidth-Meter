Bandwidth Meter 1.0.1 keeps your totals right when Bandwidth+ files, CSV
imports, or the saved store go wrong, and tidies the panel. Updating from 1.0.0
keeps everything you have recorded.

**Fixes**
- A finished day no longer shrinks. When a later Bandwidth+ read has a smaller
  total for a day and network, the larger download and upload are kept.
- `bandwidth-meter --network NAME` now filters the full summary, not only a
  single period.
- A CSV import that can't be parsed now prints the parse error.

**Safer**
- A `usage.json` that can't be read is copied to `usage.json.bak` before
  anything is saved. If that copy fails, the file is left alone. The menu bar
  and the CLI both show the error.
- When a Bandwidth+ history file can't be read, the panel says so and keeps
  showing the last saved totals.

**Panel**
- The total stands on its own, without the "METER" label or the box around
  it. Import, Export, and Quit stay at the bottom, and a failed import or
  export shows its error there.
- The monthly quota bar uses the download colour and turns red after
  90 percent.
- Settings labels are in sentence case, and Done is a button.
